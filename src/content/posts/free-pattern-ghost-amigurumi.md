## Finished size (design target)

About 6 in (15 cm) tall including hem flare, at the gauge below, lightly stuffed in the upper body.

## Gauge

14 sc and 16 rounds = 4 in (10 cm) in continuous sc.

## Materials

- Worsted weight yarn in white or cream: about 70 yd (64 m) ([yarn weight chart](/yarn-weight-chart))
- Black embroidery floss or fingering scrap for face
- US G/6 (4.0 mm) hook ([hook size chart](/crochet-hook-size-conversion-chart))
- Stuffing, yarn needle, stitch marker

## Abbreviations (US)

sc, hdc, dc, sc2tog, rnd, st(s), ch, sl st. See [abbreviations chart](/crochet-abbreviations-chart).

## Notes

- Spiral rounds for the body; mark the first stitch.
- Embroider the face before the final decrease if you close the bottom.
- Open-bottom version: skip closing rounds and work the hem onto the live stitches.

## Pattern — closed toy version

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

**Rnd 2.** 2 sc in each st. (12)

**Rnd 3.** *Sc, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (18)

**Rnd 4.** *Sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (24)

**Rnd 5.** *Sc in next 3 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (30)

**Rnds 6–18.** Sc around. (30)

Mark a stitch around Rnd 8–9 for eye height.

**Rnd 19.** *Sc in next 4 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (36)

**Rnd 20.** Sc around. (36)

**Rnd 21 (scallop hem).** *Sc in next st, hdc in next st, 3 dc in next st, hdc in next st, sc in next st, skip next st; rep from * around, adjusting at the end to land evenly. Join with sl st if desired.

Stuff upper two-thirds. For a closed base instead of an open hem, omit Rnds 19–21 and instead decrease from Rnd 18: *sc in next 3, sc2tog* around, continue decreasing to close, stuffing as you go.

## Face

With black, embroider two oval eyes at the marked height, about 4 sts apart. Optional: one small curved mouth centered below.

## Tips

- Soft stuffing in the hem keeps the scallops drapey.
- For hanging, install a ch-10 loop at the magic-ring center before stuffing closes access.
- Glow yarn as an accent round under the hem is optional and often scratchy — test a swatch.
