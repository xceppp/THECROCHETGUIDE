## Finished size (design target)

- Spider body: about 2.5 in (6.5 cm) long without legs
- Web: about 8 in (20 cm) across after light blocking

## Gauge

Firm sc fabric for the spider; web gauge is not critical. Aim for chain spaces that lie flat when pinned.

## Materials

- Worsted yarn: black (A) about 40 yd (37 m); white or pale gray (B) about 50 yd (45 m)
- US G/6 (4.0 mm) hook
- Stuffing, yarn needle, pins, stitch markers
- Optional: small wooden dowel or branch for hanging

Charts: [hooks](/crochet-hook-size-conversion-chart), [yarn weights](/yarn-weight-chart), [abbreviations](/crochet-abbreviations-chart).

## Abbreviations (US)

sc, hdc, dc, inc (2 sc in same st), rnd, ch, sl st, st(s).

## Spider abdomen (A)

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

**Rnd 2.** Inc around. (12)

**Rnd 3.** *Sc, inc; rep from * around. (18)

**Rnd 4.** *Sc in next 2, inc; rep from * around. (24)

**Rnds 5–8.** Sc around. (24)

**Rnd 9.** *Sc in next 2, sc2tog; rep from * around. (18)

Stuff.

**Rnd 10.** *Sc, sc2tog; rep from * around. (12)

**Rnd 11.** Sc2tog around. (6)

Close and weave in.

## Spider head (A)

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

**Rnd 2.** Inc around. (12)

**Rnd 3.** *Sc, inc; rep from * around. (18)

**Rnds 4–5.** Sc around. (18)

**Rnd 6.** *Sc, sc2tog; rep from * around. (12)

Lightly stuff. Sc2tog around to 6, close. Sew head to one end of abdomen.

## Legs (A) — make 8

Join with sl st at side of abdomen. Ch 14. Work sl st in each ch back to body. Fasten off; weave end into body.

Attach four legs along each side, grouped toward the head half of the abdomen. Match chain counts exactly.

## Eyes

Two French knots or small satin eyes on the head with white or red scrap.

## Web (B)

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 8 sc, join. (8)

**Rnd 2.** *Ch 5, sl st in next sc; rep from * around. (8 loops)

**Rnd 3.** Sl st into first loop. *Ch 6, sl st in next loop; rep from * around.

**Rnd 4.** *Ch 7, sl st in next ch-space; rep from * around.

**Rnd 5.** *Ch 8, sl st in next ch-space; rep from * around.

**Rnd 6.** *Ch 9, sl st in next ch-space; rep from * around.

Fasten off. With yarn needle and B, add 8 straight radial threads from outer edge to center, tacking at each ring. Block flat.

## Assembly

Sew spider off-center on web. Add ch-20 hanging loop at top of web, or sew web to a dowel.

## Tips

- If the web cups, your chains are too tight — go up a hook size for the web only.
- Sturdier legs: work 4-sc tubes for 10 rnds instead of chains.
