## Finished size (design target)

| Measurement | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Height, to the hat brim | about 5 in (13 cm) |
| Height, to the tip of the hat | about 7 in (18 cm) |
| Base width | about 3 in (7.5 cm) |

## Gauge

14 sc and 16 rounds = 4 in (10 cm) in continuous sc. A firm fabric that does not show stuffing matters more than the number.

## Materials

- Worsted weight acrylic ([yarn weight chart](/yarn-weight-chart)): red (A), skin tone (B), white (C), scrap of black (D), scrap of yellow or gold (E)
  - About 70 yd (64 m) A, 30 yd (27 m) B, 25 yd (23 m) C
- US G/6 (4.0 mm) hook, or size needed for a firm fabric ([hook size chart](/crochet-hook-size-conversion-chart))
- Stuffing, yarn needle, stitch marker
- For eyes: black yarn for embroidery, or 6 mm safety eyes

## Safety

<div class="warning">

**Safety eyes are not safe for children under three.** The name refers to the backing washer, not an age rating. For a baby or toddler gift, embroider the eyes in black yarn. If you do use safety eyes, fit them from the inside **before** the final decrease rounds close the head.

</div>

## Abbreviations (US)

sc, sc2tog, rnd, st(s), ch, sl st, invdec (optional invisible decrease). Full list: [abbreviations chart](/crochet-abbreviations-chart).

## Notes

- Body and head are one continuous piece. There is no neck and no seam.
- The waist comes from the sewn-on belt, not from shaping.
- Stuff the body harder than the head, or the figure leans.
- Color change technique: [changing yarn color](/changing-yarn-color-crochet).

## Pattern — Body and head (A, then B)

With A:

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

**Rnd 2.** 2 sc in each st. (12)

**Rnd 3.** *Sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (18)

**Rnd 4.** *Sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (24)

**Rnd 5.** *Sc in next 3 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (30)

**Rnds 6–14.** Sc in each st around. (30)

**Rnd 15.** *Sc in next 3 sts, sc2tog; rep from * around. (24)

Stuff the body firmly to here.

Change to B on the last yarn-over of Rnd 15.

**Rnds 16–23.** Sc in each st around. (24)

Fit safety eyes now if using them, between Rnds 18 and 19, about 4 sts apart.

**Rnd 24.** *Sc in next 2 sts, sc2tog; rep from * around. (18)

**Rnd 25.** *Sc in next st, sc2tog; rep from * around. (12)

Stuff the head, more lightly than the body.

**Rnd 26.** Sc2tog around. (6)

Fasten off, weave through the front loops of the remaining sts, pull closed.

## Nose (B)

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

**Rnd 2.** 2 sc in each st. (12)

**Rnds 3–4.** Sc in each st around. (12)

**Rnd 5.** Sc2tog around, stuffing lightly. (6)

Fasten off with a long tail.

## Beard (C)

**Row 1.** Ch 13, sc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across, turn. (12)

**Rows 2–5.** Ch 1, sc2tog, sc across to last 2 sts, sc2tog, turn. (4 sts after Row 5)

**Row 6.** Ch 1, sc2tog twice. (2)

Fasten off with a long tail. This makes a tapering half-shape that sits under the nose and covers the lower face.

For a faster fuzzy beard, knot fringe strands of C in an arc across the lower face instead and trim to shape.

## Hat (A and C)

With A:

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 4 sc. (4)

**Rnds 2–7.** Sc in each st around. (4)

**Rnd 8.** 2 sc in each st. (8)

**Rnds 9–10.** Sc in each st around. (8)

**Rnd 11.** *Sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (12)

**Rnds 12–13.** Sc in each st around. (12)

**Rnd 14.** *Sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (18)

**Rnds 15–16.** Sc in each st around. (18)

**Rnd 17.** *Sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (24)

**Rnds 18–19.** Sc in each st around. (24)

Change to C.

**Rnds 20–22.** Sc in each st around. (24)

Fasten off with a long tail. Do not stuff.

**Bobble (C).** Magic ring, 6 sc, 2 sc in each st (12), 2 rnds even, sc2tog around stuffing lightly. Sew to the hat tip.

## Belt (D and E)

With D, ch 30 or enough to circle the body at the waist. Sc in the 2nd ch from hook and each ch across. Fasten off.

With E, make a small square: ch 5, sc in 2nd ch and across, then 3 more rows of sc. Fasten off.

Sew the belt around the body about a third of the way up, and the square on the front as a buckle.

## Assembly, in order

1. Embroider the eyes if you are not using safety eyes — about two thirds of the way down the face, 4 to 5 sts apart. **Count from the center outward on both sides.** Do not judge by eye; the spiral is not as symmetrical as it looks.
2. Pin the beard under where the nose will go.
3. Pin the nose centered, directly above the top of the beard.
4. Stand back a few feet. Adjust. Only then sew all three.
5. Belt around the body.
6. Hat last, brim just above the eyes.

## Tips

- Eyes too high is the single most common error, and it changes the character completely. Lower than instinct says.
- If he leans, the head is stuffed harder than the body, or the base is domed. Press the base flat before stuffing.
- The color-change jog in a spiral usually ends up hidden under the beard or the belt. Place the change deliberately at the back if it bothers you.
