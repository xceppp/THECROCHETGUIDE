## Finished size (design target)

Sizes assume the gauge below after stuffing and ribbing. Re-measure on your sample before publishing or gifting as a sized pattern.

| Version | Target height (body only) | Target width |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small | about 3 in (7.5 cm) | about 4 in (10 cm) |
| XL | about 6 in (15 cm) | about 8 in (20 cm) |

## Gauge

14 sc and 16 rounds = 4 in (10 cm) in continuous sc with yarn and hook below. Exact gauge is less critical than a firm fabric that does not show stuffing.

## Materials

- Worsted weight acrylic or wool-blend yarn ([yarn weight chart](/yarn-weight-chart)): orange (A), brown or green (B)
  - Small: about 50 yd (45 m) A, 10 yd (9 m) B
  - XL: about 160 yd (145 m) A, 20 yd (18 m) B
- US G/6 (4.0 mm) hook, or size needed for a firm fabric ([hook size chart](/crochet-hook-size-conversion-chart))
- Stuffing, yarn needle, stitch marker

## Abbreviations (US)

sc, sc2tog, rnd, st(s), sl st, ch, invdec (optional invisible decrease). Full list: [abbreviations chart](/crochet-abbreviations-chart).

## Notes

- Work the body in a spiral unless noted. Mark round starts.
- Stuff firmly before the opening gets small.
- Ribbing is finishing, not crocheted ridges.

## Pattern — Small body (A)

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

**Rnd 2.** 2 sc in each st. (12)

**Rnd 3.** *Sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (18)

**Rnd 4.** *Sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (24)

**Rnd 5.** *Sc in next 3 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (30)

**Rnd 6.** *Sc in next 4 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (36)

**Rnds 7–12.** Sc in each st around. (36)

**Rnd 13.** *Sc in next 4 sts, sc2tog; rep from * around. (30)

**Rnd 14.** *Sc in next 3 sts, sc2tog; rep from * around. (24)

**Rnd 15.** *Sc in next 2 sts, sc2tog; rep from * around. (18)

Stuff firmly.

**Rnd 16.** *Sc in next st, sc2tog; rep from * around. (12)

**Rnd 17.** Sc2tog around. (6)

Fasten off, leaving a 12 in (30 cm) tail. Weave through front loops of remaining sts and pull closed. Weave in.

## Pattern — XL body (A)

Work as Small through Rnd 6. (36)

**Rnd 7.** *Sc in next 5 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (42)

**Rnd 8.** *Sc in next 6 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (48)

**Rnd 9.** *Sc in next 7 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (54)

**Rnd 10.** *Sc in next 8 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (60)

**Rnds 11–22.** Sc in each st around. (60)

Decrease by 6 sts each rnd (*sc in next k sts, sc2tog* where k counts down from 8) until 12 sts remain, stuffing as you go. Sc2tog around to 6. Close as for Small.

## Ribbing (both sizes)

With A (or a darker orange), cut a long strand. Anchor at bottom center. Wrap vertically to top center, down through the core, out the bottom. Pull a groove. Repeat for 6 or 8 evenly spaced sections. Tie off at bottom; bury ends.

## Stem (B) — both sizes

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

**Rnds 2–5 (small) or 2–7 (XL).** Sc around. (6)

Stuff lightly. Sew to top center over ribbing gathers.

## Tips

- If the pumpkin looks tall, work 1–2 fewer even rounds next time.
- Invisible decreases neat the lower half.
- For a flatter “pie pumpkin,” add one more increase round and shorten the even section.
