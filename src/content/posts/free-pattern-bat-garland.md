## Finished size (design target)

Each bat about 3.5 in (9 cm) wide wingtip to wingtip; garland length flexible (pattern uses 5 bats on a 48 in / 122 cm cord).

## Materials

- Worsted yarn in black or charcoal: about 120 yd (110 m) for 5 bats
- Optional scrap white for eyes
- US G/6 (4.0 mm) hook
- Stuffing, yarn needle, pins
- Twine, yarn, or a long chain for the hanging line

See [hook](/crochet-hook-size-conversion-chart), [yarn weight](/yarn-weight-chart), and [abbreviation](/crochet-abbreviations-chart) charts.

## Abbreviations (US)

sc, sc2tog, hdc, dc, ch, rnd, row, st(s), sl st.

## Notes

- Make all bodies before all wings so counts stay consistent.
- Weave hanging-line tails securely; garlands take handling stress.

## Body (make 5)

**Rnd 1.** Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

**Rnd 2.** 2 sc in each st. (12)

**Rnd 3.** *Sc, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around. (18)

**Rnds 4–9.** Sc around. (18)

**Rnd 10.** *Sc, sc2tog; rep from * around. (12)

Stuff.

**Rnd 11.** Sc2tog around. (6)

Close. Leave a 10 in (25 cm) tail if you will sew bats to the line instead of threading them.

## Ears (make 2 per bat)

**Row 1.** Ch 2, 2 sc in second ch from hook. Turn. (2)

**Row 2.** Ch 1, sc in first, 2 sc in last. Turn. (3)

**Row 3.** Ch 1, sc across. Fasten off with tail. Sew near top of head, mirrored.

## Wings (make 2 per bat)

**Row 1.** Ch 9, sc in second ch from hook and across. Turn. (8)

**Row 2.** Ch 1, sc2tog, sc to last 2, sc2tog. Turn. (6)

**Row 3.** Ch 1, sc across. Turn. (6)

**Row 4.** Ch 1, sc2tog, sc to last 2, sc2tog. Turn. (4)

**Row 5.** Ch 1, sc across. Turn. (4)

**Row 6.** Ch 1, sc2tog twice. Turn. (2)

**Row 7.** Ch 1, sc2tog. Fasten off. (1)

Optional scallop on long lower edge: join yarn, *sc, hdc, dc, hdc, sc, skip 1; rep from * along edge.

Sew wings to body sides at shoulder height.

## Face

Two French knots per bat.

## Garland assembly

**Line.** Ch a length that fits your mantel (example: ch until about 48 in / 122 cm), or use twine.

Space bats about 8 in (20 cm) apart. Sew each head top to the line with multiple stitches, or thread the line through the top of the body before closing Rnd 11 (plan ahead).

Add hanging loops at both ends of the line.

## Tips

- Alternate facing direction of bats for a less stiff look.
- If wings curl, mist-block flat before sewing.
