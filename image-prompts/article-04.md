# Article 04 — How to Read a Crochet Pattern

A warning that applies to all three: this article is about reading text, and
image models cannot write. Every prompt here keeps printed matter **blurred and
illegible on purpose**. A generated pattern page with invented gibberish
lettering on it would look careless, and a generated page with *plausible* text
would be a fabricated pattern, which is worse.
