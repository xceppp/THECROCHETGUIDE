# Article 11 — Crochet Gauge

- Slug: `crochet-gauge`
- Course step 11 of 12 · category: skills
- 3 images

Tape measures carry printed numbers, and image models cannot render numbers
reliably. Every prompt here keeps the markings as **unlabelled ticks** on
purpose. Invented digits on a measuring tool would be worse than none, because
this article is about measuring accurately.

---
