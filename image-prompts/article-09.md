# Article 09 — Changing Yarn Color

- Slug: `changing-yarn-color-crochet`
- Course step 9 of 12 · category: skills
- 3 images

The only article in the set that needs two colours. Use **ivory** and
**terracotta** so the images still sit with the rest, and keep those two the
same in all three.
