# Article 18 — Bat Amigurumi: Wings, Body and Ears

- Slug: `bat-amigurumi-wings-body-and-ears`
- Halloween set · category: skills · `howTo: true`
- 3 images

Seasonal palette: **charcoal black** yarn. Everything else in the house style is
unchanged.

Same lighting problem as the spider: black yarn hides stitch definition, so
every prompt asks for strong raking light.

This article is mostly about assembly — separate pieces joined to a body — so
two of the three images are component and joining shots. No finished bat, per
the `/disclosures` promise about generated images of finished objects.
