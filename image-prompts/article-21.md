# Article 21 — How to Crochet a Gnome, Step by Step

- Slug: `how-to-crochet-a-gnome`
- Christmas set · category: skills · `howTo: true`
- 3 images

Seasonal palette: **soft grey** body, **deep red** hat, **warm tan** nose,
**cream fuzzy** beard. Everything else in the house style is unchanged.

Image 3 is a side-by-side comparison rather than a single object, because the
proportion rule it illustrates is the whole article.
