# Article 13 — Crochet Hook Size Chart

- Slug: `crochet-hook-size-conversion-chart`
- Outside the course · category: charts
- 2 images

A conversion chart lives and dies on its numbers being right, so nothing here
may carry a printed size. Both prompts forbid digits explicitly. The table in
the article is the source of truth; these images only show what a hook is.
