# Article 23 — Crochet Santa Amigurumi for Beginners

- Slug: `crochet-santa-amigurumi-for-beginners`
- Christmas set · category: skills · `howTo: true`
- 3 images

Seasonal palette: **Christmas red** body, **warm tan** head, **white** beard,
**black** for the embroidered eyes. Everything else in the house style is
unchanged.

The article's whole argument is that placement decides everything, so two of
the three images are about placement rather than stitching.

All three are process shots. No completed Santa — the finished-object photo on
the pattern page will be a real photograph of a real sample.
