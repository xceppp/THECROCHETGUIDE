# Article 25 — Christmas Granny Square Variations

- Slug: `christmas-granny-square-variations`
- Christmas set · category: skills · `howTo: true`
- 3 images

Seasonal palette: **Christmas red, deep green, cream and gold**. Everything
else in the house style is unchanged.

A granny square is a traditional public-domain motif and a worked square is a
swatch, so flat finished squares are fine here in a way a finished toy is not.

**Alt:** Two granny squares, one with red rounds directly against green and one with a cream round between them
**Caption:** Illustration. Same red, same green. The cream round between them is the only change.

```
An overhead photograph of TWO flat granny squares lying side by side on a pale
oak table, photographed together at the same distance, each filling half the
frame. Both are the same size and the same number of rounds.

The LEFT square alternates rounds of deep Christmas red and deep forest green

The RIGHT square uses the SAME red and the SAME green, but with a round of pale

The contrast between the muddy left square and the crisp right one is the
subject of the photograph.

Shot from directly overhead, bright soft daylight from the upper left, soft
```

**Check:** the red and green must be the identical shades in both squares. If the model brightens the colours on the right, the image proves the wrong thing.
