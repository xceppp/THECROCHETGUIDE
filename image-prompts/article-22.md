# Article 22 — Yarn for Christmas Crochet

- Slug: `yarn-for-christmas-crochet`
- Christmas set · category: gear
- 3 images

Seasonal palette: **red, green, cream and gold**. Everything else in the house
style is unchanged.

This is a gear article, so two of the three are flat-lays of materials rather
than hands at work — the same split as the other gear article in the set.

## Image 3 — why red and green go muddy

**Save as:** `yarn-for-christmas-crochet-3.jpg`
**Goes:** under `## Colors that make life harder`
**Alt:** Two crochet swatches, one with red against green directly and one with a cream row between them
**Caption:** Illustration. Same two colors. The cream row is the only difference.

```
An overhead photograph of TWO flat rectangular crochet swatches lying side by
side on a pale oak table, photographed together at the same distance, each
filling half the frame.

The LEFT swatch is worked in horizontal stripes of deep Christmas red and deep
forest green ALTERNATING DIRECTLY, with no other colour between them. Where the
two colours meet, the boundary is indistinct and the two dark colours blur into
each other.

The RIGHT swatch uses the SAME red and the SAME green, but with a narrow stripe
of pale cream between every red stripe and every green stripe, so each colour
is clearly separated and the stripes read as crisp distinct bands.

The contrast between the muddy left swatch and the crisp right one is the
subject of the photograph.

Both swatches are the same size, the same stitch, and lie perfectly flat.

Shot from directly overhead, bright soft daylight from the upper left, soft
clean shadows. Bright modern craft photography, sharp focus, high clarity,
natural colour, no plastic sheen. No text, no numerals, no watermark anywhere in
the image. 3:2 horizontal.
```

**Check:** the reds and greens must be genuinely the same shade in both swatches. If the model lightens the colours on the right, it proves the wrong thing.
