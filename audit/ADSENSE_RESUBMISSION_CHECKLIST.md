# Resubmission checklist

Do not request another AdSense review until the items marked **block** are done. Nothing here guarantees approval. Google's review is a human judgment of the live site.

## Block — do these before asking again

- [ ] Make and measure at least a handful of the projects the homepage actually features (pumpkin, phone case, one bag, one flat motif). Put the yarn, hook, and measured size on those pages, and replace illustration captions with photos of those objects only.
- [ ] Decide the fate of the 23 consolidate-candidate URLs in `CONTENT_AUDIT.csv`. Either give each one a real sample and a distinct technique, or merge them and 301 the extras. Do not delete URLs that may already be indexed without a redirect plan.
- [ ] Read the live site the way a reviewer will: homepage, two accessory tutorials, one course page, About, Disclosures. The first two accessory pages should not feel like the same template.
- [ ] Set `OCCASION_THEME_FORCE` back to `null` when you want the calendar to control the skin, so a review after 2 November is not still wearing Halloween.
- [ ] Confirm in AdSense that the site URL you submit is `https://www.crochetexplained.com/` and that it matches `SITE.url`, the sitemap, and `ads.txt`.

## Should be true, and is true in source today

- [x] Privacy, contact, about, terms, disclosures, and cookie settings exist and are linked.
- [x] `ads.txt` publisher id matches `ADSENSE_CLIENT`.
- [x] Ad units do not render while slot ids are empty.
- [x] Project images in Markdown carry an illustration caption.
- [x] Published pages do not link to the eight draft pattern URLs (re-check after future edits).
- [x] Disclosures no longer claim that every pattern describes a made object.

## Do not do these to "look more ready"

- [ ] Do not add articles only to raise the count.
- [ ] Do not stuff keywords into titles.
- [ ] Do not invent a crochet expert, a testimonial, or a sample that was not made.
- [ ] Do not turn on ad units with empty slot ids.
- [ ] Do not unpublish the 12-step course. Those pages are the useful core.

## After the block items, before you click submit

- [ ] Open Search Console for this property and look at indexed page count and any "Crawled — currently not indexed" rows for the October cluster. This repository cannot tell you that.
- [ ] Click every footer link on a phone-width window.
- [ ] Read one new page aloud. If you cannot follow it with a hook in your hand, it is not ready, whatever the word count is.
