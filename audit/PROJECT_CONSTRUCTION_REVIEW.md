## Five projects

### Christmas stocking — REVISE, already partly revised, still not a sample

The heel flap is 12 rows of 20. The turn is short rows. Row 1 uses 14 of the 20 (12 single crochet plus one decrease) and leaves 6. Row 2 uses 7 of the 13 just made and leaves 6 on that side. That is 6 waiting on each side, 12 in all. Rows 3–14 are 12 rows, one side stitch each. The foot setup 6+12+20+12=50, and the five decrease rounds, still add up. The page did not say the 12 were six on each side. That sentence is now on the page.

What a book cannot settle: whether those short rows cup when crocheted, whether the 12 pickup stitches match the flap edge, and whether the toe closes flat. Test: crochet the heel through row 14, count 6 live stitches and zero skipped, then the setup round of 50.

KEEP the URL. WITHHOLD only if you do not want an uncrocheted heel public. That was not done.

### Christmas tree skirt — REVISE

Row 1 is chain 39, 36 double crochet. Each later row adds 12. Row 2 uses 36 and makes 48. Row 3 uses 48 and makes 60. The checkpoints still follow +12. The page now says a joined round circle is a different object, and rows 2–4 are the test for ruffle or cup. DK page 58 does not validate this slit.

Unverified: whether 12 increases per row lie flat in this yarn. Test: crochet through row 4 and lay it on the floor.

### Bowl cozy — KEEP, with the heat limit

Chain 49 is 48 single crochet. Sixty rows at the stated gauge is the same 12 inches as the width. The page now says to measure both sides, because a different row gauge makes a rectangle, and two layers are not a hot pad. The 4-H hot pad on page 23 and the DK pot holders on page 62 are other patterns. Their heat language was not copied.

Unverified: whether four pinches of about 2 inches hold this bowl. Test: join the squares, set the bowl in, and change the pinch until the base sits flat. Do not microwave it.

### Slippers — KEEP

Chain 15 is 14 single crochet. Forty-eight rows at 5 rows per inch is 9.6 inches. The cap is 16 rows and the tab is 8. Sewn to the sides, they leave 24 sole rows, 4.8 inches, which the page already stated. The sole does not narrow. The 4-H slipper on pages 24–25 is a different construction and was not used.

Unverified: wear, grip, and whether the heel stays in. Test: sew one slipper and stand on a rug, not on tile.

### Hooded scarf — REVISE

The size section said the hood was 24 inches wide and, folded, 12 inches across the face. Chain 38 is 36 half double crochet. At 3 per inch that is 12 inches, and 30 rows is about 12 inches tall. Folding that square in half makes about a 6-inch opening. The 24-inch figure did not match the chain. The page now says so, and it tells the reader to measure a hood that fits and multiply the width by 3. It does not replace the chain with an untested 72-stitch hood. The chain-2 still does not count.

Unverified: where the back seam sits on a person. Test: sew it on a person and move the seam.
