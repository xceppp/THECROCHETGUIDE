# Premium content mapping

The five drafts were not published as new articles. Each one overlaps a page that is already in the course or the charts.

The 4-H handbook, Crochet Made Easy, and the DK book were not in this workspace. Nothing below is copied from them. The drafts' own stitch arithmetic was checked and then rewritten into the existing pages.

| Source draft | Destination | Decision | Unique value kept |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| How to Start Crocheting | `/basic-crochet-stitches/` | Integrate into the course | A 16-chain, 15-single-crochet rectangle, with a written four-row count and a fasten-off note. Supplies stay on `/what-you-need-to-start-crocheting/`. |
| Hook sizes and yarn weights | `/what-you-need-to-start-crocheting/` plus the existing hook and yarn charts | Enhance the existing buying lesson | Which way to move the hook, and a short table of what to check for a swatch, dishcloth, hat, toy, or scarf. No third conversion chart. |
| Gauge swatch | `/crochet-gauge/` and `/crochet-gauge-calculator/` | Enhance the lesson, add a tool | The 16-versus-20 width sum was already on the lesson. The calculator uses that same formula. |
| How to read patterns | `/how-to-read-a-crochet-pattern/` and `/crochet-pattern-practice/` | Enhance the lesson, add a practice page | Chain 11 equals 10 single crochet. The bracket repeat uses 6 old stitches and makes 9. |
| Uneven edges | `/crochet-turning-chain/` | Integrate into the course | A written 10 / 10 / 10 / 10 check. The page already explained the wedge. |

No new article slug was created from the drafts. Three tool URLs were added because a calculator, a counter, and a quiz cannot live inside Markdown:

- `/crochet-gauge-calculator/`
- `/crochet-stitch-counter/`
- `/crochet-pattern-practice/`

They are linked from Charts & Tools. They are not extra steps in the 12-step course.
