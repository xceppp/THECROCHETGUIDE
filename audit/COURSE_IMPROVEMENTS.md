# Course improvements

Six of the twelve lessons gained a worked check that can be verified as arithmetic. The other six were already specific, and they were not padded.

Books were not used. The 4-H handbook, *Crochet Made Easy*, and the DK complete guide are not in this workspace. Nothing below is copied from them.

| Lesson | What was added |
| --- | --- |
| `/crochet-gauge/` | If a pattern wants 16 sc in 4 inches and the swatch has 20, the fabric at the pattern's stitch count is 16/20 = 80 percent as wide, which is 20 percent narrower. |
| `/basic-crochet-stitches/` | Turning-chain link at the first half double and the first double, plus a 10-stitch row that becomes 11 when the chain is counted. |
| `/crochet-turning-chain/` | Five bad rows on a 10-stitch start end at 15 stitches. |
| `/crochet-increase-decrease/` | Round 3 of a 6-stitch circle: (sc, increase) six times uses 12 stitches and makes 6 × 3 = 18. |
| `/crochet-in-the-round/` | The same 18-stitch checkpoint, then the existing tube explanation. |
| `/how-to-read-a-crochet-pattern/` | `*2 dc in next st, sk 1 st*` across 24 stitches stays at 24, because each repeat consumes 2 and makes 2. |

Not edited in the first pass: what to buy, left-handed crochet, the abbreviations chart, color changes, weaving and blocking, and where to find patterns. Previous and next links were already generated from `LEARNING_PATH`.

A later pass filled three gaps in that second group:

- `/basic-crochet-stitches/` now shows how to make the slip knot. The old text said "make a slip knot" and then counted chains. The knot is four steps, it must slide, and it does not count as a chain.
- `/left-handed-crochet/` no longer says the whole skill is no harder. The stitches are the same. An unflipped right-handed video is the hard part, which the rest of the page already explains.
- `/crochet-abbreviations-chart/` no longer treats `dc3tog` as "a number after a stitch." `3 dc` is three stitches. `dc3tog` is one decrease. The 24-stitch repeat is 24 only when the previous row has 24 stitches.
- `/what-you-need-to-start-crocheting/` and `/yarn-weight-chart/` label the yardage tables as shopping ranges, not yarn weighed for this site.

Color changes, weaving and blocking, and where to find patterns were read again. They already teach a distinct step with a way to check the result. They were not padded.
