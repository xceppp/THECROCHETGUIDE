# Article value

## `/basic-crochet-stitches/`

Solves the first rectangle. After it, the reader can chain 16, make 15 single crochets, and tell a 14 from a 16. The page already taught the stitch. The new part is the checked exercise and the links to the counter and the turning-chain lesson. It stays useful with the ads off.

## `/what-you-need-to-start-crocheting/`

Solves "which hook with this yarn." After it, the reader knows to start from the label, trust millimeters, and move the hook when the 4-inch count is off. The hook chart and the yarn chart remain the lookup tables. This page does not repeat them.

## `/crochet-gauge/`

Solves a hat that is the right stitch count and the wrong size. The 16-versus-20 example was already here. The new diagram and the calculator link show the same direction: more stitches in 4 inches, try a larger hook. The page says the prediction is not a measurement of the piece.

## `/how-to-read-a-crochet-pattern/`

Solves a line the reader can finish without a project. Chain 11 makes 10 single crochets. The bracket example uses 6 stitches and makes 9. The practice page asks those questions back.

## `/crochet-turning-chain/`

Solves a rectangle that leans. The page already had the rule and the wedge diagram. The new part is the written 10, 10, 10, 10 check, and the counter link. A 9 is a missed edge. An 11 is an extra insertion.

None of these five drafts became a second URL for the same lesson.
