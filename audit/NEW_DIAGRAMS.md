Two new SVGs. Fourteen earlier diagrams were reused where they already matched the lesson.

| File | Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public/diagrams/pattern-repeat.svg` | `/how-to-read-a-crochet-pattern/` | `[sc in next st, 2 sc in next st] 3 times` uses 6 old stitches and makes 9. |
| `public/diagrams/hook-gauge-choice.svg` | `/what-you-need-to-start-crocheting/` and `/crochet-gauge/` | 20 stitches in 4 inches against a pattern of 16 means try a larger hook. 14 means try a smaller hook. |

Both have a title, a description, alt text, and a caption that starts with "Diagram." Labels use US terms. They are schematic, not photographs, and they are not labeled as AI illustrations.

Checked against the pages they sit on:

- 16 chains, skip 1, equals 15 single crochet. That stays in the prose. It is not a new drawing.
- 11 chains, skip 1, equals 10.
- One repeat uses 2 and makes 3. Three repeats use 6 and make 9.
- More stitches per 4 inches means a narrower fabric.

No image was taken from the reference books. Those files were not available here.
