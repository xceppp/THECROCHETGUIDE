# Educational value added in this pass

## Homepage and Start Here

A reader can reach the gauge calculator, the stitch counter, and the pattern practice without hunting through Charts. The project list stays below those tools, and it still says untested pages are drafts to check.

## Increase and decrease

The lesson already stated that `sc2tog` pulls up a loop in two stitches and closes all three loops. The new diagram shows that sequence and the count change: two stitches become one. It does not replace the written half-double and double decreases, which use different loop counts.

## Already on the site from the previous release

Those items were not redone. They remain the main teaching additions: the 15-stitch rectangle, the 10-stitch edge check, the chain-11 reading example, the bracket repeat of 6 old stitches and 9 new stitches, and the gauge calculator's 16-versus-20 example (3.2 inches instead of 4, 20 percent narrower).
