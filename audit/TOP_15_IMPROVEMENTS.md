# Top 15 article improvements

These are edit instructions, not published rewrites. Do not add stitch counts, yardage, or "I made this" lines that are not already supported by a yarn sample.

Priority is usefulness to a beginner plus how much a reviewer notices the page, not raw word count.

## 1. The 5 Basic Crochet Stitches — KEEP, still the spine

`/basic-crochet-stitches/`

- Lead with what each stitch is for (fabric, height, yarn use), which the page already does. Do not add a seasonal intro.
- Add one worked swatch photo per stitch only after someone makes the swatch. Until then, keep diagrams labeled as illustrations.
- Cross-link the turning-chain page at the first place a beginner will chain up, not only at the end.

## 2. Crochet in the round — KEEP

`/crochet-in-the-round/`

- This is the prerequisite for almost every October project. The project pages should link here before they start a magic ring, and this page should link onward only to projects that have been yarn-tested.
- Do not paste a pumpkin or ghost pattern onto this page.

## 3. Gauge — KEEP

`/crochet-gauge/`

- The page already tells readers when they can skip a swatch. Keep that.
- The accessory pages quote stitches-per-inch as "estimate." Point those estimates at this page and say the number is a planning figure, not a measured swatch, which several already do. Make the wording identical so it does not sound like a measured gauge on one page and a guess on the next.

## 4. Pumpkin, step by step — IMPROVE

`/how-to-crochet-a-pumpkin-step-by-step/`

- Distinct technique (sphere plus surface ribbing). Worth keeping as its own URL.
- The page tells the reader the counts are a shape recipe, not a finished-inch promise. Leave that.
- Human task: make one small pumpkin, write the hook, yarn, and measured size on the page, and replace the illustration caption with a photo only of that pumpkin.

## 5. Phone case — IMPROVE

`/how-to-crochet-a-phone-case/`

- Latest commit message pins this page. A reviewer who opens the homepage may land here.
- Do not name a phone model or quote a millimeter size unless it was measured on that model.
- Human task: one case, one phone, one photo of the phone inside it. Until then the illustration caption must stay.

## 6. Abbreviations chart — KEEP

`/crochet-abbreviations-chart/`

- Keep it printable and outside the "read it in order" pressure. It is a tool, which is the kind of page AdSense's "information or tools" line actually describes.
- Check that every abbreviation used in the October projects appears on this chart. Add a missing abbreviation only if it is a standard US term, not a private shorthand invented for one page.

## 7. Beginner scarf — IMPROVE

`/how-to-crochet-a-beginner-scarf/`

- A scarf is one of the few projects a beginner can finish without shaping. If the page is mostly "chain and work rows," say the finished width is whatever the chain measures, and give a row-count method (work until the scarf reaches the length you want) instead of a fake yardage.
- Do not publish a specific inch length unless it was measured.

## 8. Baby blanket — IMPROVE

`/how-to-crochet-a-baby-blanket/`

- Safety belongs here: eyelet holes a finger can pass through, long tails, and buttons. Add that only as general finishing advice, not as a test of a particular blanket.
- Do not label it a "newborn size" without a measured square.

## 9. Mug cozy — IMPROVE

`/crochet-mug-cozy-for-beginners/`

- The gift-ideas page already warns that the mug must be measured. Keep the cozy page focused on the measurement method (circumference of the mug, overlap for the button) and delete any sentence that repeats the gift-guide intro.
- Human task: one mug, one cozy, the measured circumference written on the page.

## 10. Market bag — CONSOLIDATE CANDIDATE

`/how-to-crochet-a-market-bag/`

- This is the representative of the bag cluster (market bag, mini shoulder bag, granny tote, phone crossbody, wine-bottle bag).
- Do not write five more bag pages. Pick one construction (mesh market bag **or** solid granny tote), yarn-test that one, and turn the others into short sections or links once the sample exists.
- Until that choice is made, leave the URLs up. Removing them without redirects would throw away whatever URLs are already indexed. Redirects need an explicit decision.

## 11. Ghost amigurumi — IMPROVE

`/crochet-ghost-amigurumi-for-beginners/`

- The bell shape is a real lesson (increase, even rounds, hem). Keep it separate from the ghost coin pouch and ghost candy bag, which are the same holiday noun with a different object.
- Human task: decide whether the pouch and candy bag stay. They are consolidate candidates, not this page.

## 12. Snowflake — IMPROVE

`/how-to-crochet-a-snowflake/`

- Points that cup are the actual problem. Keep the blocking note.
- The ornament roundup used to link a draft snowflake-set URL. That link was removed because the draft is not on the site. Do not link drafts.

## 13. What you need to start — KEEP

`/what-you-need-to-start-crocheting/`

- This is the gear page a beginner should see before any October project.
- Affiliate links, if any, must stay disclosed at the top of the page. Do not add product names that are not already verified.

## 14. Christmas stocking — IMPROVE

`/how-to-crochet-a-christmas-stocking/`

- A heel or a folded rectangle is a different project. The page should say which one it is in the first paragraph.
- Human task: if the heel is only described, either write the counts that are already on the page more clearly or mark the heel as "not included" so the headline does not promise a shaped heel the instructions skip.

## 15. Halloween granny square variations — IMPROVE

`/halloween-granny-square-variations/`

- This page reuses the color-change lesson, which is the right kind of internal link.
- Keep colorways as examples. Do not add a second granny tutorial that only swaps Christmas colors without a new technique. The Christmas granny page should state what is different (palette only, or a different join). If it is palette only, say that in the first paragraph so the two pages are not twins.

## What not to do to these 15

- Do not pad them to a word target.
- Do not add an author anecdote.
- Do not generate new "finished object" images.
- Do not unpublish them in this pass.
