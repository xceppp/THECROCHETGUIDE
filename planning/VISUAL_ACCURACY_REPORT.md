## What was kept, replaced or removed

- **Kept:** all 241 remaining illustrations. The earlier library pass
  (`planning/FULL_LIBRARY_PROGRESS.md`) already reviewed every illustration
  against its corrected text and removed the ones that contradicted it, for
  example the lip balm image with the ring at the base, the ghost image
  stuffed through an open hem, and posed in-use shots of finished objects.
  Those files remain on disk but are not on any page.
- **Captions that disclose a mismatch:** where an illustration shows a
  different finish from the written pattern, the caption says so. Example:
  the mug cozy's lead image "draws a textured stripe and a round button tab.
  This page makes plain single crochet with a chain loop."
- **Spot check this pass:** the illustrations on the lace bandana, scrunchie,
  mug cozy and black cat wallet pages were viewed next to the new step
  diagrams. None contradicts the diagrams or the counts.
- **Replaced:** none this pass. The step diagrams were added beside the
  illustrations, not instead of them.
