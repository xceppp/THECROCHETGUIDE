# Unverified pattern limitations

Date: 2026-10-11. No pattern on this site has been crocheted by the
publisher. This file says what that means for each kind of page, so nobody
reads "checked" as "tested".

## What "checked" means here

- Every round and row count was added up on paper: each repeat times its
  stitches equals the stated total, and each round uses exactly the stitches
  of the round before. Round charts in the step diagrams are generated by code
  that refuses to draw a round whose arithmetic disagrees with the text.
- Sizes are arithmetic from an example gauge (usually 4 sc and 5 rows per
  inch) and published standards: ID-1 card 3.370 x 2.125 in (ISO/IEC 7810),
  US bill 2.61 x 6.14 in, the Craft Yarn Council hook, yarn and head charts.
- Nothing about fit, drape, stretch, durability, heat, washing, or how a
  piece looks when made has been observed.

## Known limits by project type

| Type | Examples | What paper cannot tell you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pockets sized to a card or device | All wallets, phone case, earbud and bat pouches, gift card holder, laptop sleeve, cable organizer | Whether the item slides in after seaming. The pages tell the reader to try the real card or device in the flat or folded piece before seaming |
| Drawstring bags | Ghost coin pouch, ghost candy bag, pumpkin treat bag, makeup pouch | How tightly the eyelet round cinches in a given yarn |
| Wearables | Beanie, ear warmer, gloves, slippers, hooded scarf, bandana, scrunchie | Fit on a real head, hand or foot, and how ribbing relaxes. The hooded scarf page states outright that its old 12-inch hood will not fit an adult |
| Stuffed amigurumi | Ghost, Santa, gnome, bat, pumpkin, spider, tree | Final size and how firmly it stands. Stuffing amount changes both |
| Flat motifs | Coasters, granny squares, snowflake, flower, leaf, star, heart | Whether a circle stays flat in a particular yarn and tension. Pages explain cupping and ruffling |
| Heat and kitchen items | Bowl cozy, mug cozy, coasters | Heat protection. The bowl cozy page forbids microwave and hot-pad use |
| Blankets and scarves | Baby blanket, beginner scarf | Size after washing. The blanket page requires a washed swatch |

## What would lift a limitation

A made sample, photographed, with its measured gauge and finished size
recorded on the page. Until then, a reader's photo through `/contact/` is the
only evidence a pattern works, and the template already invites it.
