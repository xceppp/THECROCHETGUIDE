# Final editorial review

Date: 2026-10-11. Reviewer: the agent that made the changes, so read it as a
self-check, not an independent review.

## Accuracy

- Every count drawn in a new diagram was checked against its article first.
  Round charts are generated by code that throws an error on a mismatch.
- Errors found and fixed this pass:
  - Tote: "a count that is not a multiple of 12 means a cluster went into a
    stitch top" overstated the case. A misplaced cluster can keep the count.
    Now: "a cluster was added or missed, often because one went into a stitch
    top instead of a space."
  - Granny round diagrams (tote and coasters) described round 1's corners and
    round 2's side spaces incorrectly. Fixed.
  - The BLO rib caption gave the wrong stretch direction. Fixed.
  - Santa hat: "and so on" replaced with rounds 8 to 16, 15 up to 27, each
    verified.
- A caption written for the ghost mini wallet gave a reason for its ch-5 loop
  that the article does not give. It was replaced with the article's own
  advice (ch 6 or ch 7 if the loop misses).
- No construction errors were found in the patterns themselves.

## Honesty about testing

- 49 of 51 pages with counts carry a near-top statement that they were
  checked on paper and not test-crocheted. The other two hold practice
  swatches only. Four statements were added this pass.
- 241 illustrations are labeled as AI illustrations. Where a picture differs
  from the pattern, the caption says how.
- The author is described as a publisher and editor who checks instructions,
  never as a maker.

## Voice and style

- US terms and US spelling. No emoji. No exclamation marks in new text.
- New text follows the site voice: short declaratives, the useful thing
  first, uncertainty stated plainly.
- Filler scan: 0 matches across 69 articles.

## Structure

- 69 of 69 articles have a Frequently asked section. 66 have a mistakes or
  troubleshooting section. The three without one are the gift ideas round-up,
  the free pattern sources guide, and left-handed crochet, which covers its
  problems under "Practical notes".
- 49 articles are within 1,000 to 1,800 words. 20 are longer, all patterns or
  pattern-adjacent pages. See `CONSOLIDATION_RECOMMENDATIONS.md`.
