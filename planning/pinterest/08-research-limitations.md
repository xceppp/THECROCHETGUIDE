# Research limitations

Checked on October 9, 2026. Scores are opportunity estimates for US Pinterest clicks. They are not impressions, clicks, or revenue.

## Verified

- All 65 published articles returned HTTP 200 and appear in `https://www.crochetexplained.com/sitemap-0.xml`. Eight draft files were excluded. Non-article pages were excluded.
- Pinterest Predicts 2026, published December 9, 2025 on the Pinterest Newsroom, names two crochet-relevant ideas: crochet play mats under Throwback Kid, and crocheted phone cases plus softly stitched bandanas under Laced Up. The newsroom page does not give a search-volume percentage for those two crochet phrases. Comfort is described as a 2026 consumer theme. Vamp Romantic is a named 2026 beauty aesthetic, not a crochet query.
- Pinterest Business Community, October 2, 2026, “Cozy Harvest Moon,” cites trends.pinterest.com for: mini pumpkin painting ideas +100%, rainy fall outfits +100%, caramel apple bar ideas +40%, October nail ideas +900%, and Harvest Moon activities +2,000. The last figure is printed as “+2,000,” not “+2,000%.” These are adjacent seasonal searches, not crochet keywords.
- Pinterest Business Community, September 18, 2026, cites fall centerpieces for table +40% and baby costumes +30%, among other non-crochet searches.
- Pinterest’s 2026 hobbies note, via the Business Community in September 2026, says 8 in 10 monthly Pinterest users say Pinterest motivates them to try new things, and it frames screen-free hobbies. It does not publish a crochet search volume.
- eRank’s Q3 2026 Etsy roundup ranks “halloween,” “christmas,” “crochet pattern,” and “crochet” in the Etsy top 20. That is Etsy search rank, not Pinterest volume.
- The account baseline supplied for this plan is fewer than 100 impressions and zero outbound clicks. That sample cannot judge creative.

## Inferred, not measured

- Halloween projects are the best October use of the pumpkin, centerpiece, and costume-adjacent signals.
- Rainy fall outfits +100% is a reason to pin beanies, scarves, ear warmers, gloves, and hoods in October, before deep winter.
- Christmas ornaments, stockings, and gift tutorials are scheduled as rising, not peaking. A December 5, 2025 Pinterest community note cited ornament decor +130%. That is last holiday season, so it is historical context only.
- US gift-making usually starts before Thanksgiving. Thanksgiving 2026 is November 26. The 30-day calendar ends November 7, so it covers Halloween and the start of gift planning, not Christmas week.
- Charts and stitch lessons are useful, but a pin can satisfy the lookup without a click. Their demand and click scores are estimates, and most sit in band D.

## Unavailable

- trends.pinterest.com was not opened directly. Percentages above come from Pinterest’s own community posts that cite that tool.
- No Google Trends interest curve was available for these crochet keywords on October 9, 2026. The public US daily trend list that day was not about crochet.
- No Pinterest Analytics export was available. Pin URLs in the tracker are blank until a pin is published.
- Existing pins for these articles were not on file. The inventory marks them as none on file.
- Third-party pages that quote very large crochet growth rates, including “crochet bat patterns +1,500%,” were not treated as verified Pinterest data.

## Scoring

| Criterion | Points | How it was applied |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Current US seasonal relevance | 25 | Halloween now, rainy-day wear and early gifts next, charts low |
| Search demand | 20 | Estimate, except phone case and bandana, which Pinterest named, and a few adjacent official trends |
| Visual appeal | 20 | Clear finished object scores higher. One illustration, or a chart, scores lower |
| Click motivation | 20 | A make-it tutorial scores higher than a chart the pin can replace |
| Beginner usefulness | 10 | Beginner practical pages score higher. Intermediate pages score lower |
| Article and image quality | 5 | Complete pages score 4. Thin image sets and safety-limited pages score 3 |

Bands: 85–100 A, 70–84 B, 50–69 C, below 50 D. Result: 4 A, 25 B, 25 C, 11 D.

## Pin rules already baked in

- Every pin URL is the matching article, not the homepage.
- Design A is the finished object. Design B is a teaser. Neither invents a row count or calls the work yarn-tested.
- Bowl cozy pins say the page does not promise microwave safety.
- Slipper pins say crochet soles slide on hard floors.
- The ornament article still links to two unpublished drafts, `free-pattern-mini-christmas-trees` and `free-pattern-snowflake-set`. Pins for that article go only to the live ornament URL.
- No website files were changed.

## Tracker math

Outbound CTR = outbound clicks / impressions × 100.

If impressions are 0, leave CTR blank. Do not divide by zero, and do not record 0% as if the pin were measured.
