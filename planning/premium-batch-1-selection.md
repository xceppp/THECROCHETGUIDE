# Premium batch 1 selection (second run)

Chosen from published articles on 2026-10-11. The first run of this batch (beginner scarf, baby blanket, ear warmer, simple flower, book sleeve) is already live at `c663d3c`. Its selection, report, and matrix are preserved in that commit. Those five, the bowl cozy, the hooded scarf, and all drafts are excluded here.

The 4-H Crocheting Handbook, Crochet Made Easy, and the DK guide were opened from `Downloads\NCCONSULTING` and used only to confirm principles: the DK flat circle (starts at 8 and doubles, with a 6-stitch pillow circle elsewhere), the DK note that a single-crochet seam leaves a ridge best kept on the wrong side, and the 4-H mitten cuff worked flat in back-loop rows with the hand picked up from the row ends. No pattern, prose, or diagram from them was reproduced.

## Why these five

Each one teaches a construction the others do not, and each had a fault that can be shown on paper.

| Article | Construction | Why it is in this batch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/how-to-crochet-a-lace-bandana/` | Granny triangle worked in rows | The page said the work starts at the point. It starts at the middle of the long edge. Row 2 was listed as 9 dc and has 12. Three illustrations show motifs the pattern does not make. |
| `/how-to-crochet-fingerless-gloves/` | Flat rib cuff, tube in rounds, thumb gap | The cuff called 28 rows 7 inches at 4 rows per inch while the page's gauge said 5. The thumb round chained 6 over 4 skipped, adding 2 stitches without saying so. Round 1 picked up one stitch per row end, which flares. |
| `/free-pattern-halloween-coasters/` | Flat circle, plus a granny square | The circle was given as rows to follow with no explanation of why 6 increases, why circles cup or ruffle, or how to resize. Square and dishcloth sizes contradicted the tote page. |
| `/how-to-crochet-a-granny-square-tote/` | Joined panels, edge, straps | The joining counts were right but scattered. The top edge had no count, the strap spacing had no reason, and the yardage was not weighed. Four illustrations show a boxed bag or a different motif. |
| `/crochet-mug-cozy-for-beginners/` | Sideways strip with a closure | Rows go around the mug, but the length was worked from the stitch gauge, 36 rows instead of 45. The seamed option said to stitch above and below the handle, where the handle is attached. Several paragraphs were about filming. |

## Considered and left out

- **Scrunchie:** sound already, and its construction is simple. A rebuild would mostly add length.
- **Chunky beanie, slippers, amigurumi:** the crown, sole, and shaping need a made object before they can be taught responsibly.
- **Market bag, phone crossbody:** their base and mesh counts need a sample to confirm they lie flat.
