# Premium batch 1 selection

Chosen from published articles on 2026-10-11. The bowl cozy and the hooded scarf are excluded because they are already the benchmark. Drafts are excluded, including the Christmas stocking and the Christmas tree skirt.

The 4-H handbook, Crochet Made Easy, and the DK guide are not in this workspace. They were not opened. Stitch definitions used in the rewrites are ordinary US terms. Head sizes, where an article needs them, come from the Craft Yarn Council head-circumference chart.

## Why these five

They are short, they teach different constructions, and each one can be checked on paper. They are not five versions of the same pouch.

| Article | Why it is in this batch |
| --- | --- |
| `/how-to-crochet-a-beginner-scarf/` | A first rectangle. The old page gave 24 stitches and 150 rows from one unverified gauge, and it buried the fact that the same 24 stitches become 9.6 inches if the swatch is looser. |
| `/how-to-crochet-a-baby-blanket/` | Same stitch as the scarf, different problem: a large chain, a washed gauge, and things that must not be sewn onto a baby blanket. The 90-stitch example only works at 3 stitches per inch. |
| `/how-to-crochet-an-ear-warmer/` | The chain is the width and the rows are the circumference. The old page copied the stitch gauge into the row gauge and treated a 2 inch ease cut as a fit. |
| `/how-to-crochet-a-simple-flower/` | A flat motif in the round, not a rectangle. The petal formula is checkable. The old page spent its length on filming and color advice. |
| `/how-to-crochet-a-book-sleeve/` | A folded rectangle sized from an object. The fold doubles the height. The old page stated the 22 by 60 counts without showing that 3/4 of the book height, times 2, times the row gauge, is where 60 comes from. |

## Considered and left out

Amigurumi, slippers, beanies, and the phone case need a made object before their shaping can be taught as a pattern. Making those pages longer would hide that. They wait for a later batch or a sample.
