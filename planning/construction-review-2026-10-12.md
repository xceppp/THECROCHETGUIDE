## Confirmed fixes

**Spiderweb wallet.** Removed every “button near row 10” placement from the closure diagram, its description, and the article alt text. The step now says to insert the cards, close the flap without stretching it, mark where the loop lands, remove the cards, and sew the button through the front wall only. `spiderweb-wallet-closure.svg` draws the flap, the chain-8 loop, the mark, and a needle that stops in the front wall.

**Pumpkin stem.** Removed “roll from a short end.” The stem is 6 stitches by 3 rows. The drawing and the article roll the 6-stitch width so the two 3-row edges meet. The 3-row direction is the cylinder height. The stem is sewn upright on the back rim over the tab base. The tab folds over the opening on its own.
